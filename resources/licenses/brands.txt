Database marks (resources/icons/db, src/shared/db-brand-paths.ts)

The glyph shapes of PostgreSQL, MySQL, SQLite, ClickHouse, Redis, MongoDB and Elasticsearch
come from the "simple-icons" package (https://simpleicons.org), released under CC0 1.0
(public domain dedication). The licence covers the drawing files, not the marks.

The names and logos of these products are trademarks of their respective owners. DataNest is
not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by them. The marks are used only to identify
the database a connection talks to (nominative use). The SQL Server and Oracle marks are
simple drawings of ours.
